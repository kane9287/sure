# Alpha Feedback Template

Copy this for each individual friction point. One item per report — small and specific beats long and vague.

```
What I was trying to do:


What I expected to happen:


What actually happened:


How confusing/annoying was it? (1 = barely noticed, 5 = stopped me cold):


Screenshot or exact page (optional):

```

## Example, filled in

```
What I was trying to do:
Move $50 from my Dining envelope to cover an overspent Groceries envelope.

What I expected to happen:
Pick Groceries as the source, type $50, done.

What actually happened:
Wasn't clear which category was "source" vs "destination" from the labels alone —
had to guess.

How confusing/annoying was it?
2

Screenshot or exact page:
/budgets/1/budget_categories, the "Move money" section under Groceries
```

Even a "this felt fine, no notes" is useful to hear — it tells us a flow is working, not just where it's broken.
