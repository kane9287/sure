# Glossary

Every term used in the app, in one place. Alphabetical.

**Account** — Anything with a balance: a bank account, credit card, loan, investment account, or manually-tracked asset like a house or car.

**Asset account** — An account that represents something you own (checking, savings, investments, property). Balance goes up on income, down on spending.

**Available (to spend)** — In Envelope Mode: what's left in a category's envelope right now (assigned minus spent, net of refunds). Can go negative if you've overspent.

**Budget** — The monthly plan/view for a family, in either Cap or Envelope mode.

**Budget Mode** — The family-wide setting that determines whether the budget page works as Cap Mode or Envelope Mode. Changed in Settings → Preferences → Budgeting.

**Cap Mode** — A limit-based budgeting mode: you set a spending cap per category and an expected income, and the app tracks actual spend against that plan. See [Budgeting](02-budgeting.md#cap-mode).

**Category** — A label describing what a transaction was for (Groceries, Rent, Dining, Paycheck). Categories can have subcategories.

**Connected account** — An account linked to an outside data provider (e.g. Plaid, SimpleFIN) so balances/transactions sync automatically, instead of being entered by hand.

**Envelope** — In Envelope Mode, the "bucket" of real money assigned to a single category. Money is funded into it from Unassigned, spent from it by transactions, and carries forward month to month.

**Envelope Mode** — A cash-based budgeting mode: every dollar of real cash gets assigned ("funded") to a category envelope before it's considered available to spend. See [Budgeting](02-budgeting.md#envelope-mode).

**Fund / Funding** — In Envelope Mode, the act of moving money from Unassigned into a category's envelope.

**Goal** — A savings target tied to one or more accounts (e.g. an emergency fund). Money allocated to a goal is earmarked and subtracted from Unassigned, similar to an envelope.

**Holding** — In an investment account, the current position in a specific security (how many shares/units you own).

**In-budget account** — An account marked as included in Envelope Mode's cash calculations (its balance counts toward Unassigned). Accounts can be excluded if you don't want them factored into day-to-day budgeting.

**Liability account** — An account representing money you owe (credit card, loan, mortgage). Works "backwards": a positive balance means you owe money; new charges increase it, payments decrease it.

**Overspent** — In Envelope Mode, when a category's Available balance has gone negative — you've spent more than was assigned to it.

**Pending (transaction)** — A transaction a provider has reported but that hasn't fully settled/posted yet. Shown with a "Pending" badge; amount/details can still shift slightly before it posts for good.

**Reconciliation / Update balance** — Correcting an account's balance to match reality (your real bank statement) by using the account's "Update balance" action. The app automatically creates the adjustment behind the scenes — it does not count as a spending transaction. See [Good Habits](03-good-habits.md#if-the-ledger-is-off-dont-dwell-on-it).

**Rule** — An automation that applies a category (or other change) to matching transactions automatically, going forward and optionally to past transactions too.

**Sync** — The background process that pulls new balances/transactions from a connected provider. Runs automatically; can usually be triggered manually from the account page.

**Tag** — An optional label you can apply across categories (e.g. "Vacation 2026") for grouping transactions that a single category can't describe.

**Trade** — A buy/sell (or similar) activity in an investment account that changes a Holding.

**Transaction** — A single movement of money: Expense (money out), Income (money in), or Transfer (between your own accounts).

**Transfer** — A transaction type for money moving between two of your own accounts. Doesn't count as income or spending.

**Unassigned** — In Envelope Mode, the pool of real cash in your budget accounts that hasn't yet been funded into any category envelope or goal. Always derived from actual balances — never a typed-in or projected number.

**Uncategorized** — The synthetic bucket for transactions that don't have a category assigned yet. Not an error — just a "needs attention" state.
